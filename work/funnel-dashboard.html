Key takeaways
  • Mobile checkout is the biggest fixable leak: mobile cart→checkout 50% vs 65% on desktop; worth ~₹26 L/year if we close half the gap.
  • The one-page checkout works: +0.61 pp conversion (+17%, p = 0.001); mobile checkout→payment +8.4 pp. Recommendation: roll out.
  • Slow pages (>3s) halve conversion on mobile and desktop alike; a third of mobile sessions are slow.
  • Paid social brings 22% of traffic but the lowest revenue per session; fix landing pages / targeting.
